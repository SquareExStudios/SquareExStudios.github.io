Tell Us About Your Space

To begin, we need a few key details about your exhibition space. Think of it as a blank canvas—once we understand its basic layout and dimensions, we can start creating a tailored concept for you.
-Booth Type & Details: Let us know your booth number, its overall size and dimensions (e.g., 3m x 3m), and how many sides are open to the aisle.
-Structure: We need to know if you have a pre-built structure (a "Shell Scheme") or if you have an empty floor space to work with ("Space Only").
-Floor Plan: Please share the official floor plan for your booth if you have it.
Once we have these details, we can move on to designing a booth that perfectly fits your needs.

Designing Your Vision

Once we have the details of your space, our design team will get to work. We'll use your requirements and goals to create a stunning, customized booth concept.
-Creative Concept: We'll present you with a visual design for your booth, including 3D renderings and layouts, so you can see exactly what it will look like.
-Detailed Proposal: Along with the design, we'll provide a detailed proposal that includes a breakdown of materials used.
-Feedback & Revisions: We'll work with you to refine the design until it perfectly matches your vision and meets all your needs for the event.
