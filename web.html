WEBSITE DESIGN

Planning, Design and Development, Launch and Maintenance.

Discovery. Information Architecture. Wireframes. Design. Content. Development. Deploy.

Strategy & Design

In this initial phase, we work with you to define the vision for your website. This is where the foundation for everything that follows is built. We'll focus on understanding your business, your audience, and your goals to create a blueprint for success.
-Discovery: We start with a conversation to understand your project's purpose and what you want to achieve.
-Planning: We'll outline the pages your website needs and how users will navigate through them. This creates a "sitemap" that acts as our roadmap.
-Visual Design: Our team creates a unique and professional design for your website. We'll show you mockups and layouts, so you can see how your new site will look and feel before we start building it.

Development & Building

Once you approve the design, we bring it to life! This is the "construction" phase where our developers write the code to create a fully functional website. We handle all the technical details so you don't have to.
-Coding: We translate the approved design into a working website, using the latest technologies to ensure it's fast, secure, and works perfectly on all devices (desktops, tablets, and phones).
-Content Integration: We integrate your text, images, and videos into the website, making sure everything is in its proper place and looks great.
-Testing: We rigorously test every part of the website—from buttons and links to forms and functionality—to ensure everything works as it should. We make sure the site is bug-free and ready for launch.

Launch & Support

The final step is to make your website live and available to the world. But our job doesn't end there—we're here to make sure your site continues to run smoothly and stay up-to-date.
-Launch: We handle the technical process of putting your website online, making sure it's live and accessible to your customers.
-Search Engine Optimization (SEO): We implement basic SEO practices to help search engines like Google find and understand your website from day one.
-Training & Handover: We provide a simple tutorial on how to update your website's content yourself (if applicable), giving you full control. We also offer ongoing support and maintenance services to keep your site secure and performing at its best.
