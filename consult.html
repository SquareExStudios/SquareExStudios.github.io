Discovery & Strategy

This initial phase is all about understanding the client's needs, goals, and current situation across all relevant service areas.
-Initial Consultation & Needs Assessment:
--Purpose: To gather high-level information about the client's business, their goals (short-term and long-term), their target audience, and their current challenges.
--Process: A meeting (virtual or in-person) to discuss the client's vision for their events, their online presence, and their marketing objectives.
-Deep Dive & Requirements Gathering:
--Purpose: To delve into specific details for each service area, identify exact requirements, and conduct any necessary research.
--Process: Follow-up meetings, questionnaires, competitive analysis (for digital marketing/website), and technical audits (for existing websites).

Solution Development & Design

This phase focuses on creating the specific solutions and designs based on the agreed-upon strategy and requirements.

-Concept Development: We create initial design concepts and plans for your event booth, website layout, and digital marketing campaigns.
-Client Collaboration: We refine these designs based on your feedback through multiple rounds of revisions and collaboration.
-Final Approval: We finalize all plans and designs, securing your approval to move into the implementation phase.

Execution & Optimization

This final phase is where the plans are put into action, and ongoing monitoring and refinement ensure success.
-Implementation & Launch: We build and produce the event materials, develop the website, and launch all digital marketing campaigns.
-Post-Launch Support: We provide on-site support for events, ongoing maintenance for the website, and active management of marketing campaigns.
-Performance Reporting: We continuously monitor key metrics, analyze the results, and provide recommendations for future improvements.
